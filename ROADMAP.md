# Icon roadmap

Concepts still to draw, in rough priority order. Names are generic; this list carries no codes from any proprietary standard.
Each one is researched first (`scripts/research.mjs`), then drawn at five sizes in both styles.

## Done in batch 1
Renewable energy, marine shipping, rail, capital markets, asset management, consumer finance, hospitals, sovereigns, media, packaging, paper & forest products, IT services.

## Industries
- **Materials:** construction materials, containers, steel, gold & precious metals, industrial gases, fertilisers
- **Industrials:** building products, electrical equipment, conglomerates, distributors, airlines, air freight & logistics, airports, ports, toll roads, security services, staffing, environmental services
- **Consumer:** auto parts, motorcycles, consumer electronics, home furnishings, homebuilding, appliances, leisure products, apparel & luxury, footwear, casinos & gaming, hotels & cruise lines, restaurants, broadline retail, home improvement retail
- **Staples:** drug retail, food retail, beverages, brewers, packaged foods, tobacco, household products, personal care
- **Health care:** medical devices, health care supplies, managed care, health care technology, life sciences tools
- **Financials:** payments, exchanges & data, mortgage finance, brokers, reinsurance, life insurance, property & casualty insurance
- **Technology:** communications equipment, hardware & storage, electronic components, semiconductor equipment
- **Communications:** wireless, advertising, broadcasting, cable & satellite, publishing, games, interactive media
- **Utilities:** multi-utilities, independent power
- **Real estate:** office, industrial, retail, residential, hotel, health care, data centre, tower, timber and self-storage property; real estate services

## Credit
- **Structured finance:** auto loans, credit card receivables, student loans, equipment, aircraft, covered bonds, CDOs, single-family rental
- **Public finance:** states, counties, cities, school districts, water & sewer, toll roads, airports, ports, stadiums, higher education, housing
- **Sovereigns & supranationals:** supranationals, local and regional governments
- **Funds:** money market funds, pension funds, closed-end funds
- **Infrastructure & project finance:** power projects, social infrastructure, PPP

## Depth rule
Top two levels of each standard always get an icon; deeper only for visibly distinct children (SPEC §9). Next distinct children to draw: forestry, fishing, livestock.
