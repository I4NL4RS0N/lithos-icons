Icons for finance.

Sectors, industries, credit asset classes, countries and regions. Every icon is drawn at five sizes, in outline and solid, and chosen from research into what people already recognise.

Icons and code are MIT. Country and region outlines are generated from Natural Earth (public domain); regions follow UN M49. Most flags are adapted from flag-icons (MIT). Pilot build: shapes and content will change before 1.0.
